## Recording history

The user selected Voice 3 from three ElevenLabs Voice Design previews on
14 September 2026. That voice was saved in the user’s ElevenLabs account as
**SyncApp Coach**. These English clips were generated with that saved voice
using Eleven v3 and downloaded through ElevenLabs. They are AI-generated speech,
not a recording of a real person and not a celebrity voice clone.

A single take contains ten countdown numbers followed by Go, Rest, Start and
Workout complete. Each clip was separated at silent gaps, with 40 ms of leading
padding, 80 ms of trailing padding and 4 ms edge fades. No pitch shifting or
time stretching was applied. Numbers are shorter than one second so timing
remains controlled by the workout clock.

The original decoded take’s SHA-256 is: `117d473ca3ebb093e8db1fc8525367813e53185ce012f4b1f2e40079bbd7c9b6`.

The generated audio remains subject to the user’s ElevenLabs account terms and
applicable plan; this file does not grant a separate license.
